Chemotherapy refers to the administration of anti-neoplastic agents or the use of intracellular poisons that have an anti-neoplastic effect. Strictly speaking, the term chemotherapy excludes the use of targeted therapies, hormonal therapies, or immunoöncotherapy.
Chemotherapy regimens with the intent of cure are often multi-drug therapies given in cycles.

# Induction
Induction refers to the first line treatment with chemotherapeutic agents. The intention is often cure.

# Intensification

# Maintenance
After induction, with or without consolidation, some patients continue on a maintenance regimen. These protocols utilise drugs with a more favourable toxicity profile with the goal of prolonging progression free survival.
