Acute phase reactants are markers of inflammatory processes. They may be increased in acute inflammatory states (positive) or deceased (negative).

# Positive reactants
- [[C-reactive protein|CRP]]
- Platelets
- Ferritin
- Fibrinogen

# Negative reactants
- Albumin
- [[Transferrin]]
